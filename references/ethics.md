# EcoVadis — Ethics Theme Reference

## Activated Criteria (typical)
- Corruption and bribery prevention
- Responsible information management (data privacy)
- Anti-competitive practices
- Whistleblowing and grievance mechanisms
- Conflicts of interest
- Responsible tax practices (large companies)
- Political contributions (if applicable)

## Key Evidence Documents

### Code of Conduct — Required Elements
- CEO/Board endorsement (explicit signature)
- Covers: anti-corruption, conflicts of interest, gifts/hospitality, data privacy, competition law, whistleblowing
- Applies to: all employees, officers, agents, third parties
- Sanctions for violations stated
- Acknowledgement / sign-off process for employees

### Anti-Corruption Policy — Required Elements
- Zero-tolerance stance on bribery and facilitation payments
- Gifts, entertainment, and hospitality thresholds
- Government official interaction procedures
- Third-party due diligence (agents, JV partners)
- Record-keeping requirements
- Reference to local laws (FCPA, UK Bribery Act, etc.)

### Whistleblower / Speak-Up Policy — Required Elements
- Anonymous reporting channel (hotline, web platform, or named officer)
- Non-retaliation / non-reprisal guarantee (explicit)
- Investigation process described
- Escalation path and timelines
- Board or Audit Committee oversight

### Data Protection / Privacy Policy — Required Elements
- Compliance with applicable laws (GDPR, DPDP, etc.)
- Categories of personal data collected
- Purposes and lawful bases for processing
- Data retention periods
- Data subject rights (access, deletion, etc.)
- Data breach notification process
- DPO or responsible contact named

### Document Templates Structure
**Code of Conduct**:
```
1. Message from Leadership
2. Our Ethical Principles
3. Anti-Corruption and Bribery
4. Conflicts of Interest
5. Gifts, Hospitality and Entertainment
6. Competition and Fair Dealing
7. Responsible Use of Company Resources
8. Data Privacy and Confidentiality
9. How to Raise a Concern
10. Non-Retaliation Commitment
11. Acknowledgement
[CEO signature + date]
```

### Actions Evidence
- Anti-corruption training records (completion logs with dates)
- Third-party due diligence records or process documentation
- Whistleblower case log (even nil return is evidence of tracking)
- Data privacy impact assessment (DPIA) or record of processing activities (RoPA)
- Employee CoC acknowledgement / e-sign records
- Internal audit scope covering ethics topics

### KPI Examples for Results Pillar
| KPI | Notes |
|---|---|
| % employees trained on CoC / anti-corruption | Annually, with completion rate |
| Whistleblower reports received / investigated / resolved | Even nil is useful |
| Third-party due diligence checks completed | # per year |
| Data breaches reported / resolved | With timelines |
| Conflicts of interest declared | # per year |

### High-Value Signals
- **UN Global Compact signatory** — Publicly listed, directly references ethics + all 10 principles
- **ISO 37001** — Anti-bribery management system (rare but very strong)
- **Transparency International membership**
- **Ethics or Compliance Officer** — Named role signals governance maturity

## Quick Wins for Score 40–50 → 60+
1. Upload training completion logs from ANY compliance/ethics training in the past 2 years
2. Make sure CoC is CEO-signed and dated — unsigned drafts don't count
3. Add a formal whistleblower policy if it's currently buried in the handbook
4. Join UN Global Compact (free for SMEs) and upload the commitment letter
5. Create a simple third-party due diligence checklist and upload as evidence of a process

## Data Privacy Note (Tech/Software Companies)
For tech companies (like SaaS or cloud providers), EcoVadis activates data privacy criteria more heavily. ISO 27001 certificate + privacy policy + GDPR DPA template together are very strong evidence for this criterion.
