# EcoVadis — Labour & Human Rights Theme Reference

## Activated Criteria (typical)
- Health, safety and working conditions
- Working hours and wages
- Child labour and forced labour prevention
- Non-discrimination and equal opportunity
- Freedom of association / collective bargaining
- Employee development and training
- External stakeholder human rights (community)
- Diversity and inclusion

## Key Evidence Documents

### L&HR Policy — Required Elements
- Reference to ILO Core Conventions
- Commitment to fair wages, working hours, safe conditions
- Non-discrimination statement (race, gender, religion, disability, age, etc.)
- Prohibition of child and forced labour — explicitly
- Freedom of association acknowledgement
- Scope covering all own employees + where possible, contractors

### Health & Safety Policy — Required Elements
- Senior management accountability
- Commitment to prevent accidents, injuries, and occupational illness
- Named H&S Officer or responsible role
- Risk assessment process referenced
- Incident reporting and investigation procedure
- Reference to ISO 45001 if certified

### Equal Opportunity Policy — Required Elements
- Non-discrimination grounds listed explicitly
- Grievance and complaint procedure referenced
- Commitment to equal pay / gender pay review
- Accommodation for disabilities

### Actions Evidence
- Training completion records (with dates, topics, attendee counts)
- H&S incident log / near-miss register (even if zero incidents)
- Internal or third-party H&S audit reports
- Performance review / L&D programme documentation
- Diversity data (gender split, management representation)
- Workplace satisfaction or engagement survey results

### High-Value Certifications
- **ISO 45001** — H&S management system (very strong)
- **SA8000** — Social accountability (labour practices)
- **SEDEX / SMETA audit** — Social audit report

## Quick Wins for Score 40–50 → 60+
1. Upload training logs from any recent H&S or ethics training
2. Document H&S incident data — even nil returns count as evidence of tracking
3. Add a grievance procedure section to the Employee Handbook
4. Reference ILO conventions explicitly in the L&HR Policy
5. Publish any workforce diversity data (even basic gender split)

## Modern Slavery Statement
UK-registered companies >£36M turnover legally require this. EcoVadis treats it as strong evidence for child/forced labour criteria. Include:
- Business structure and supply chains
- Due diligence process
- Risk assessment approach
- Training provided
- KPIs / effectiveness measures
- Future steps
