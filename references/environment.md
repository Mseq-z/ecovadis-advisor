# EcoVadis — Environment Theme Reference

## Activated Criteria (typical)
- Energy consumption & efficiency
- Greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions
- Water consumption
- Waste generation & management
- Air pollution / emissions
- Natural resource consumption
- Biodiversity & land use
- Product use & end-of-life (if applicable)
- Customer H&S (product safety, if applicable)

## Key Evidence Documents

### Environmental Policy — Required Elements
- Senior management signature + date
- Scope: which sites, subsidiaries, activities
- Commitment to: GHG reduction, energy efficiency, waste minimisation, legal compliance
- Named responsible person (e.g., Sustainability Manager, EHS Director)
- Specific targets (quantified where possible): "Reduce Scope 1+2 GHG by 30% by 2030 vs 2022 baseline"
- Annual review commitment
- Reference to ISO 14001 if certified

### Environmental Policy — Template Structure
```
1. Purpose and Context
2. Scope of Application
3. Our Environmental Commitments
   3.1 Climate and GHG Emissions
   3.2 Energy Management
   3.3 Water Stewardship
   3.4 Waste and Circular Economy
   3.5 Biodiversity and Natural Resources
4. Governance and Accountability
5. Legal Compliance
6. Continuous Improvement and Targets
7. Communication
8. Review
[Signature block]
```

### GHG Inventory Guidance
EcoVadis rewards companies that report:
- **Scope 1**: Direct emissions (owned vehicles, on-site combustion, refrigerants)
- **Scope 2**: Purchased electricity/heat (market-based or location-based)
- **Scope 3**: Value chain emissions (begin with upstream categories 1–4; downstream later)
- Year-over-year comparison data (at least 2 years)
- Intensity metrics (e.g., tCO2e per €M revenue, per employee)
- Alignment with GHG Protocol methodology

### KPI Examples for Results Pillar
| KPI | Unit | Ideal Reporting Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Total GHG emissions (Scope 1+2) | tCO2e | Annual |
| GHG intensity | tCO2e / €M revenue | Annual |
| Total energy consumption | MWh | Annual |
| Renewable energy share | % | Annual |
| Water withdrawal | m³ | Annual |
| Waste generated | tonnes | Annual |
| Waste diversion rate (recycling) | % | Annual |
| Environmental non-conformances | # | Annual |

### High-Value Certifications
- **ISO 14001** — Environmental management system (strongest signal)
- **ISO 50001** — Energy management
- **CDP disclosure** — Climate, water, forests (very strong for results)
- **Science-Based Targets (SBTi)** — Signals commitment + ambition
- **EcoVadis Carbon Action Module** — Direct integration with scoring

## Quick Wins for Score 40–50 → 60+
1. Publish a GHG inventory (even Scope 1+2 only)
2. Set a named emissions reduction target with a year
3. Document an energy audit or metering programme
4. Add environmental KPI section to any existing annual report
5. Reference ISO 14001 formally in Environmental Policy even if certification is in progress

## Common Gaps at Score 50
- Policies exist but no quantitative targets
- No GHG data uploaded — only stated intentions
- Missing Scope 2 electricity reporting
- No sustainability report (even a 4-page PDF counts)
- Biodiversity not mentioned at all
