# EcoVadis — Cross-Cutting Foundations Reference

## The Strategic Multiplier Documents

These documents, done well, provide evidence across multiple themes simultaneously and are worth prioritising if you're near the 55-document upload limit.

---

## 1. Sustainability / CSR Report

**Value**: Can satisfy Results pillar evidence across ALL four themes in a single document.

### Recommended Structure (GRI-Lite for SMEs)
```
Executive Summary
  - CEO message with ESG commitments
  - Key performance highlights (KPIs)

About This Report
  - Reporting boundary (entities/geographies covered)
  - Reporting period
  - Reference to GRI Standards (even partial)
  - Third-party assurance (if available)

Our Business and Strategy
  - Materiality assessment summary
  - SDG alignment

Environment
  - GHG emissions (Scope 1, 2 — Scope 3 if available)
  - Energy consumption and renewable share
  - Water consumption
  - Waste and recycling rates
  - Targets and progress

Labour & Human Rights
  - Workforce data (headcount, gender, region)
  - H&S metrics (LTIR, training hours)
  - Employee development
  - D&I data and initiatives
  - Modern slavery statement reference

Ethics
  - Anti-corruption training completion
  - Whistleblower programme summary
  - Data privacy compliance summary

Sustainable Procurement
  - Supply chain sustainability overview
  - Supplier assessment data (# assessed, % coverage)
  - Supplier development initiatives

Appendix: GRI Index (even partial table)
```

**Minimum viable length**: 8–15 pages (quality > quantity)

## 2. Board Governance Documentation

**Value**: Elevates the Policies pillar across all themes — signals sustainability is board-level, not just operational.

### What to Document
- Board resolution or charter clause that sustainability/ESG is a board responsibility
- Named board committee responsible (e.g., Audit & Risk Committee, standalone ESG Committee)
- Frequency of ESG reporting to board (quarterly recommended)
- Executive-level KPI tied to sustainability outcomes (e.g., ESG in CEO remuneration)

### Template: Board Resolution
```
RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
[Company Name]

Date: [DD Month YYYY]

WHEREAS the Board recognises that sustainable business conduct is integral to long-term value creation;

RESOLVED THAT:
1. The Board affirms its oversight responsibility for the Company's ESG strategy and performance.
2. The [Audit & Risk Committee / ESG Committee] is delegated responsibility for reviewing ESG performance on a [quarterly] basis.
3. The CEO is accountable for implementing the Company's sustainability commitments, with ESG performance forming part of the annual performance review.
4. The Company shall publish an annual sustainability report disclosing performance against key ESG metrics.

Signed: [Chair of the Board]
Date: [DD Month YYYY]
```

## 3. UN Global Compact Communication on Progress (COP)

**Value**: Highly regarded third-party endorsement. Free to sign up. Directly references all 10 Principles covering human rights, labour, environment, and anti-corruption.

### COP Outline
```
1. Statement of Continued Support (CEO-signed)
2. Human Rights Principles (1–2): Actions taken + outcomes
3. Labour Principles (3–6): Actions taken + outcomes
4. Environment Principles (7–9): Actions taken + outcomes
5. Anti-Corruption Principle (10): Actions taken + outcomes
6. Measuring Progress: KPIs for each area
```
Publish on the UNGC website; upload the PDF to EcoVadis.

## 4. Materiality Assessment

**Value**: Demonstrates strategic maturity. EcoVadis rewards companies that have formally identified their material ESG topics.

### Simple Materiality Matrix Approach
1. List 15–20 ESG topics relevant to your industry
2. Rate each on two axes: (a) Importance to stakeholders, (b) Impact on business
3. Plot on a 2x2 matrix
4. Describe the engagement process used (surveys, interviews, workshops)
5. State top 5–8 material topics and how they map to your strategy

## Document Upload Strategy (55-Document Cap)

### Priority Tier 1 — Must Upload
- Sustainability / CSR Report (covers everything)
- ISO certificates (all held)
- Core policies (Environmental, L&HR, H&S, CoC, Supplier CoC, Whistleblower, Data Privacy)

### Priority Tier 2 — High Value
- Training completion records
- GHG inventory / energy data
- Supplier assessment results
- H&S incident logs

### Priority Tier 3 — Fill Remaining Slots
- Board resolution on ESG governance
- Materiality assessment
- UN Global Compact COP
- External audit reports
- Diversity data

### De-prioritise
- Generic company brochures (no evidence value)
- Marketing materials
- Duplicate documents covering same topic
- Drafts or unsigned versions
